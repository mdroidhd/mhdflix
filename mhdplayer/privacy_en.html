📜 Mhdplayer Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2025

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used and protected within the Mhdplayer application, developed by Leonardo Alexander Soto.

By using the application, you accept the terms described in this policy.

1. Information we collect

Mhdplayer may collect certain technical data necessary for its proper functioning and to display advertisements. This data may include:

  • Device identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID).
  • IP address and operating system version.
  • General usage information (frequency of use, errors, performance).

👉 We do not collect personally identifiable information such as name, email, contacts, photos or videos of the user.

2. Access to files and local content

The application requests permission to access the device's multimedia files (videos or music) for the sole purpose of allowing their local playback within the player.

  • None of the files accessed are collected, stored or transmitted to external servers.
  • Mhdplayer does not upload, analyze or share the user's multimedia content.

3. Advertising

Mhdplayer uses the Wortise SDK to display ads within the application. Wortise and its partners may collect non-sensitive information, such as the advertising ID, to offer personalized or non-personalized ads, according to the user's settings.

You can obtain more information about data processing by Wortise in their official Privacy Policy:

🔗 https://www.wortise.com/privacy-policy

4. User control over advertising data

The user can limit or disable ad personalization by following these steps on their Android device:

Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID or Disable ad personalization.

Mhdplayer respects the user's settings and will adjust ad delivery accordingly.

5. Data usage

The collected information is used only to:

  • Maintain the correct functioning of the application.
  • Improve stability and performance.
  • Display relevant advertisements (if consent has been given).

6. Data sharing

Mhdplayer does not share personal data with third parties, except for anonymous data used by the advertising provider (Wortise), necessary for the functioning of advertisements.

7. Security

We adopt reasonable measures to protect data against unauthorized access or disclosure. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8. External links

The application may contain links to external websites (e.g., Wortise policy). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of such sites.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Any important changes will be notified in the application or on this page with an updated revision date.

10. Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at:
